Gripper keys: Q opens, E closes. Space freezes or unfreezes all controls.

Joints

Shaded area is the allowed range. If a joint moves the wrong way, tick “invert”. Limits are also set in the Arduino sketch.

Saved poses

Go moves the arm smoothly to the pose. Move the arm where you want it, then press Save to store it. Poses are remembered in this browser.
